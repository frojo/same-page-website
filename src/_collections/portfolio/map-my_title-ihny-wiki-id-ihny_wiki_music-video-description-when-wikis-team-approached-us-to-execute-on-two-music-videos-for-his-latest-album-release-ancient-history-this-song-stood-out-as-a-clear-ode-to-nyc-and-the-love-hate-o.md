description: "When Wiki's team approached us to execute on two music videos for
  his latest album release \"Ancient History\", this song stood out as a clear
  ode to NYC and the love // hate one can hold in their hearts simultaneously
  towards this city. We wanted to execute this piece on two fronts: a visual
  reference to older public access news networks reminiscent of a lost time on
  television, and a guide through beautiful cityscapes this place has to offer.
  We aimed to capture the artist's humor, resentment, and love in this piece."
